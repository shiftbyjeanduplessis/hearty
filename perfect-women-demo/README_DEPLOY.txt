PERFECT WOMEN — SIX-MONTH POPULATED DEMO

Deploy the entire folder named perfect-women-demo.
Suggested live URL:
  https://hearty.health/perfect-women-demo/

This is separate from the real app and uses a separate local-storage key, so it will not overwrite client data in /perfect-women/.

The demo includes:
- fictional client Megan Jacobs
- 26 weekly weigh-ins across six months
- monthly measurements
- 30 days of water and movement logs
- weekly coaching check-ins
- completed Walking and Foundation Home history
- 12 weeks of populated gym strength sessions
- progressive-overload working weights and next-session recommendations
- start, month-3 and month-6 front/side/back progress photos
- all exercise images

The demo data resets whenever the page is refreshed, so visitors cannot permanently damage it.
The app clearly labels the account and progress photos as fictional demo data.
