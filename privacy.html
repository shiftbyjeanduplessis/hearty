Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

Hearty is built as a practical GLP-1 companion app. This policy explains the basic privacy position for this Phase 1 release.

Information stored on your device

Many app items are stored locally in your browser/app storage, including theme preferences, meal logs, hydration logs, support-mode choices, exercise logs, progress photos and similar daily tracking data.

Photos and medication details

Progress photos, meal photos, medication type, dose and detailed injection history are intended to remain on your device unless a future version clearly asks for permission and adds the correct privacy controls.

Account and access information

Where account access is used, Hearty may use Supabase authentication to help you sign in and keep access linked to your purchase email.

Support requests

If you email or message support, we will receive the information you choose to send in that message so we can help you.

No selling of personal data

Hearty is not built around selling personal data or showing ads.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@hearty.health.
