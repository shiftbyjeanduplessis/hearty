Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 June 2026

What Hearty is

Hearty is a digital GLP-1 companion app for routines, meal planning, hydration, exercise, progress tracking and support-mode check-ins. For privacy questions, contact support@hearty.health.

Information you may provide

Depending on the features you choose to use, Hearty may process information such as your email address, profile and setup details, meal preferences, body metrics, hydration and progress entries, medication or injection schedule details, symptom/support check-ins, app settings, and customer-support messages.

Photos and Community posts

  • Progress photos taken for your private progress tracking are intended to remain on your device unless you intentionally choose to share content elsewhere.
  • If you use Community/Social features and choose to post a profile image, message or photo, that shared content may be uploaded so it can be shown in the community feature.

Payments

Payment information is processed by the payment provider shown during checkout. Hearty may retain purchase-related details necessary to assist with access, billing queries, refunds and fraud or dispute handling.

How information is used

  • To provide and personalise the Hearty app features you choose to use.
  • To help with account access, customer support and purchase questions.
  • To maintain service reliability, security and product improvement.
  • To display community content only when you choose to participate or share.

Customer support and AI

Hearty currently offers customer contact through email and WhatsApp. If an AI Help Assistant is introduced, this policy will be updated to explain what support messages are processed through that tool before it is offered publicly.

Your choices

You may contact us to request help accessing, correcting or deleting account-related information held for the service. Some data saved only on your device may need to be removed from that device by you, and may not be recoverable after browser/app data is cleared.

Medical boundary

Hearty is not a medical service. Do not use customer support or the app as a replacement for medical advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
