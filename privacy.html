Privacy

How Hearty Hub uses your information

When you request a plan, we use the details you enter to calculate and deliver that plan. This may include your name, email, age, height, weight, goal, food preferences and appetite selections.

What we use it for

We use the information to create your requested plan, save your funnel progress on your device, send the requested plan, and improve the funnel using anonymous event data. Marketing messages are optional.

Automatic carry-over

On the same browser and device, your completed plan can be stored temporarily and loaded into Hearty. The transfer expires and should not be used on a shared device.

Your choices

You can start over to clear the saved plan state. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

For privacy questions, contact info@hearty.health.

Hearty Hub provides general food, weight-planning and routine guidance, not medical advice. Follow the guidance of your healthcare professional.
