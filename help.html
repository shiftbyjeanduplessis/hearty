I opened Hearty online but cannot find the app

Hearty runs online and can be added to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

iPhone
  1. Open Hearty in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App when shown, then tap Add.
Android
  1. Open Hearty in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Add to home screen, then Install.
