Greek yoghurt protein bowl

Protein: ±30g • Calories: ±350 kcal

Ingredients

  • Greek yoghurt
  • Protein powder or high-protein yoghurt
  • Berries
  • Small sprinkle of oats or seeds

Method

  1. Mix yoghurt and protein powder if using.
  2. Top with berries and a small amount of oats or seeds.
Low-appetite version:
Use half the yoghurt and eat the rest later.

Egg and cottage cheese plate

Protein: ±28g • Calories: ±320 kcal

Ingredients

  • 2 boiled eggs
  • Cottage cheese
  • Cucumber or tomato
  • Salt, pepper or seasoning

Method

  1. Boil eggs ahead and keep chilled.
  2. Plate with cottage cheese and simple salad veg.
Low-appetite version:
Eat one egg first and keep the cottage cheese for later.

Chicken yoghurt lettuce cups

Protein: ±32g • Calories: ±360 kcal

Ingredients

  • Cooked chicken
  • Greek yoghurt or light mayo
  • Lettuce cups
  • Cucumber or pickle

Method

  1. Mix chicken with yoghurt or light mayo.
  2. Spoon into lettuce cups and keep portions small.
Good for:
Low appetite, warmer days, no-cook lunches.

Lean mince bowl

Protein: ±35g • Calories: ±450 kcal

Ingredients

  • Lean mince
  • Cooked veg
  • Small portion rice, potato or beans
  • Tomato relish or seasoning

Method

  1. Cook mince with seasoning and tomato.
  2. Serve with veg and a small carb portion.
Low-appetite version:
Eat the mince and veg first. Save the rest for later.

Cottage cheese snack plate

Protein: ±22g • Calories: ±250 kcal

Ingredients

  • Cottage cheese
  • Crackers or toast
  • Cucumber, tomato or fruit

Method

  1. Plate cottage cheese with a small crunchy side.
  2. Use as a mini-meal when a full plate is too much.

Chicken and veg soup

Protein: ±28g • Calories: ±330 kcal

Ingredients

  • Cooked chicken
  • Broth or stock
  • Soft vegetables
  • Optional small potato or noodles

Method

  1. Simmer everything until soft.
  2. Serve a small bowl and repeat later if needed.
