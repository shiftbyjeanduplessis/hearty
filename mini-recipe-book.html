Breakfast

Greek yoghurt protein bowl

5 minutesNo cooking

Greek yoghurt, berries, seeds and optional protein powder.

25–35 g protein
Ingredients + method

Ingredients: 200 g Greek yoghurt, ½ cup berries, 1 tsp seeds and optional protein powder.
Method: Spoon into a bowl, add the toppings and mix gently.

Low appetite
02
Breakfast

Egg and cottage cheese plate

10 minutes5 ingredients

Eggs, cottage cheese, tomato or cucumber and toast if useful.

28–34 g protein
Ingredients + method

Ingredients: 2 eggs, ½ cup cottage cheese, tomato or cucumber and 1 slice toast.
Method: Cook the eggs as preferred and serve everything on one plate.

Very easy

30–40 g protein
Ingredients + method

Very easy

Tuna and cracker plate

Tuna, yoghurt or light mayo, cucumber and crisp crackers.

Dinner

Lean mince bowl

20 minutesOne pan

Lean mince, potato or rice, vegetables and tomato-based sauce.

30–40 g protein
Ingredients + method

Ingredients: 120 g lean mince, ½ cup tomato sauce, 1 cup vegetables and ½ cup rice or potato.
Method: Brown the mince, add sauce and vegetables, then serve with the starch.

Very easy
06
Dinner

Chicken traybake

30 minutesMakes leftovers

Chicken, mixed vegetables, baby potatoes and simple seasoning.

30–40 g protein
Ingredients + method

Ingredients: 120–150 g chicken, 1½ cups vegetables, 4 baby potatoes, oil spray and seasoning.
Method: Roast together at 200°C until the chicken is cooked and vegetables are tender.

Very easy
