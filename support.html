Knowledge & tools

Small, manageable steps for harder days.

When appetite is low

Choose smaller meals and start with a simple protein option you tolerate. Your Meals page will prioritise gentler choices while Support Mode is active.

When nausea is getting in the way

Keep food simple and take fluids in small amounts if that feels easier. Contact your healthcare professional for persistent, severe or concerning symptoms.

Movement on difficult days

Support Mode reduces exercise expectations. Rest or stop if you feel unwell; keeping safe matters more than completing a session.
