Why this belongs to USN

GLP-1 creates a new customer behaviour. USN already owns the categories around it.

People on GLP-1 medication are not only buying “weight-loss products”. They are trying to protect muscle, hit protein, stay hydrated, move safely and keep nutrition consistent despite a smaller appetite. That is exactly where USN’s product ecosystem and education can become more valuable.

P

Protein becomes the anchor

When appetite drops, protein intake becomes harder. The app can help users understand protein targets, meal structure and suitable USN product options.

H

Hydration becomes daily behaviour

Hydration is a practical daily habit, not a one-off purchase. The app turns this into tracking, reminders and useful product education.

M

Muscle protection becomes the message

USN can lead with responsible strength, movement and nutrition support rather than thinness-focused messaging.

The app system

A daily companion that connects the user back to USN every day.

The strongest version is not a generic tracker with a USN logo. It is a branded support system that helps the GLP-1 user make better daily decisions — and gently introduces relevant USN ranges at the exact moment they make sense.

04

CEO marketing angle

USN can become the support brand for the GLP-1 era.

The opportunity is not to compete with doctors or clinics. It is to own the daily lifestyle conversation after the prescription: protein, hydration, movement, education, progress and product discovery.

Marketing concept only

This is a brand and campaign idea, not an operational rollout plan. The immediate value is to show USN how the GLP-1 customer can be reached with a relevant, responsible digital support message.
