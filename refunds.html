Billing & refund help

Effective date: 4 June 2026

Please contact us if something went wrong

If you paid for Hearty but cannot access it, cannot find the installed app, cannot update your information or believe you were charged incorrectly, contact us so we can investigate and help resolve the problem.

What to include

  • The email address used during checkout.
  • Your country and payment method, if known.
  • A short description of the access, billing or product issue.

Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed based on the purchase and issue raised. Nothing on this page limits any consumer rights that apply to you under applicable law or through your payment provider.

Please do not pay twice

If you have purchased but cannot access Hearty, do not make a second purchase to try to restore access. Contact support first.
