How to use Hearty

Hearty is built around a simple daily rhythm: meals, water, movement, progress and Support Mode. You do not need to use everything perfectly. Start with one or two basics and build from there.

Start with these five steps

  1. Open Home to see today’s rhythm.
  2. Generate or view meal ideas in Meals.
  3. Log water and meals when you can.
  4. Use Support Mode on rougher days.
  5. Add progress updates when you are ready.

Your daily rhythm

Home shows what you have logged today and gives simple next steps like logging meals, adding water, checking Support Mode or recording movement.

Open Home

Meals

Meals gives practical meal ideas, approximate protein estimates, snacks, rough-day backups, shopping lists and meal logging. Protein amounts are estimates based on standard serving assumptions.

  • Generate my week
  • View today and full week
  • Refresh snack ideas
  • Use rough-day backups
  • Log or save meals
Open Meals

Support Mode

Support Mode is for days when appetite, energy or meals feel harder. You can check in with low appetite, nausea, bloating, exhaustion or protein struggle. Hearty can then keep ideas simpler where possible.

Open Support

Progress

Progress helps you review signals over time, including weight logs, progress photos, habit rhythm and milestones. Photos are optional. Add them only when you are ready.

Open Progress

Movement

Exercise is for simple movement and beginner-friendly strength support. You can log a walk, light movement or a simple strength session. Small movement counts.

Open Exercise

Settings

Settings is where you manage account access, support contact, app preferences, theme, water target, meal preferences and install help.

Open Settings

Install Hearty on your phone

On iPhone

  1. Open Hearty in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

On Android

  1. Open Hearty in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm.

What Hearty is not

Hearty is not medical software. It does not diagnose symptoms, prescribe treatment, adjust medication or replace your doctor, pharmacist, dietitian or coach. Hearty is a practical daily support app for tracking, meal ideas, hydration, movement, progress and rough-day support.

Need help?

If you are stuck, cannot access your account or something does not look right, contact support.
