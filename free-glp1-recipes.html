20 simple GLP-1 recipes.

Protein-first ideas for normal days, busy days and low-appetite days. Adjust portions and ingredients to suit your needs and medical guidance.

1

Greek yoghurt protein bowl

Ingredients: 200g Greek yoghurt, berries, 1 tbsp seeds, optional protein powder

Method: Stir together and serve cold.

2

Egg and cottage cheese plate

Ingredients: 2 eggs, 100g cottage cheese, tomato or cucumber, toast if wanted

Method: Cook the eggs and serve with cottage cheese and vegetables.

3

Chicken protein bowl

Ingredients: Cooked chicken, rice or potato, mixed vegetables, light dressing

Method: Build a small bowl and add dressing just before eating.

4

Tuna crunch wrap

Ingredients: Tuna, plain yoghurt, cucumber, lettuce, small wrap

Method: Mix tuna with yoghurt, add vegetables and roll tightly.

5

Lean mince and potato bowl

Ingredients: Lean mince, potato, peas or spinach, tomato-based sauce

Method: Brown the mince, add sauce and vegetables, then serve over potato.

6

Protein smoothie

Ingredients: Milk or soy milk, Greek yoghurt or protein powder, banana, ice

Method: Blend until smooth. Sip slowly on low-appetite days.

7

Chicken noodle soup

Ingredients: Chicken, broth, soft vegetables, a small portion of noodles

Method: Simmer until the vegetables are soft and add shredded chicken.

8

Prawn stir-fry bowl

Ingredients: Prawns, stir-fry vegetables, rice, soy sauce

Method: Cook prawns and vegetables quickly, then serve with rice.

9

Chicken meatballs

Ingredients: Lean chicken mince, herbs, breadcrumbs, tomato sauce

Method: Shape into small meatballs, bake, and simmer briefly in sauce.

10

Low-appetite snack plate

Ingredients: Cheese, yoghurt, crackers, fruit and sliced chicken or turkey

Method: Use small portions and eat in stages rather than forcing a large meal.

11

Cottage cheese toast

Ingredients: Wholegrain toast, cottage cheese, sliced tomato, seasoning

Method: Toast the bread and top with cottage cheese and tomato.

12

Salmon and soft potato

Ingredients: Salmon fillet, baby potatoes, green beans, lemon

Method: Bake salmon and potatoes; serve with steamed beans and lemon.

13

Turkey and avocado roll-ups

Ingredients: Turkey slices, avocado, cucumber sticks, soft cheese

Method: Spread, roll and slice into easy bite-sized pieces.

14

Tofu rice bowl

Ingredients: Firm tofu, rice, edamame, cucumber, light soy dressing

Method: Pan-cook tofu and serve over rice with vegetables.

15

Overnight protein oats

Ingredients: Oats, Greek yoghurt, milk, chia seeds, berries

Method: Mix and refrigerate overnight. Add a splash of milk before eating.

16

Chicken and hummus pita

Ingredients: Chicken, hummus, lettuce, tomato, small pita

Method: Fill the pita with hummus, chicken and vegetables.

17

Beef and vegetable stew

Ingredients: Lean beef, carrots, potato, broth, herbs

Method: Slow-cook until the beef and vegetables are tender.

18

Egg fried rice

Ingredients: Eggs, cooked rice, peas, spring onion, soy sauce

Method: Scramble eggs, add rice and peas, then season lightly.

19

Soft fish cakes

Ingredients: Canned salmon or tuna, mashed potato, egg, herbs

Method: Combine, shape into small cakes and pan-cook until golden.

20

Yoghurt berry freezer bites

Ingredients: Greek yoghurt, berries, optional peanut butter

Method: Spoon small clusters onto a tray and freeze until firm.
