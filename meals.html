MEALS GUIDE

How this plan works

Your plan is built around your prep model: cook fresh, dinner leftovers, or weekly bulk prep. Portions and prep steps sit inside each meal card.

Pick a prep model

Cook as you go gives fresh meals. Dinner → next lunch cooks dinner as two portions. Weekly bulk prep saves a separate prep plan with a 3-day repeat cycle.

What to cook

Each meal shows a quick Cook / serve line. Open “View portion + prep” for exact portion guides and simple cooking steps.

Bulk prep

Bulk prep is saved as your weekly prep plan. It shows batch recipes, portions to cook, container count, labels, and simple storage advice.

Macros

Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre are estimates per meal and per day. They are a guide, not strict tracking.

Snacks

Snacks are optional protein top-ups. Eat one if you need it, skip it if you do not.

Build new weekly plan

Use “Build new weekly plan” when you want a new set of meals. In Weekly bulk prep mode, “Refresh prep plan” only refreshes the saved prep plan, containers and packing guidance for the current meals.
