MEALS GUIDE

How Meals Works

Your meals are built to help you get enough protein, keep eating simple, and support you through GLP-1 appetite changes.

Daily plan

Each day gives you breakfast, lunch, dinner, and optional snacks if snacks are enabled.

Portions

  • Protein = 1 portion
  • Starch = 1 portion
  • Vegetables = 1–2 portions
  • Yoghurt = 1 cup
  • Fruit = 1 fruit or 1 small serving

Snacks

Snacks are optional. Use them when appetite is low, protein is difficult, or you need something small between meals.

Swaps

You can swap meals when needed. Try to swap like-for-like: protein for protein, starch for starch, and snack for snack.

Leftovers

If leftovers are enabled, dinner may become the next day’s lunch.

Support Mode

Support Mode is for harder GLP-1 days. When appetite is low or side effects are active, meals may become smaller, simpler, softer, or snack-based for the day. Turning Support Mode off should return you to your standard meal plan.

Shopping list

Use the shopping list after generating your weekly meals. You can download or copy it without leaving the app.
