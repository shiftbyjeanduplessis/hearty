Quick questions

Before you use the free plan

Can I use this if I’m on Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound, or Mounjaro?

Hearty is a practical meal-planning tool for simple food ideas, portions and structure. Medication, side effects and medical conditions should be discussed with your doctor, pharmacist or dietitian.

What if I have very low appetite most days?

The plan is designed around simple, protein-focused meals that can be easier to manage on lower-appetite days. Choose foods you actually tolerate and use the plan as a practical starting point.

Is this medical or nutrition advice?

No. This tool provides general nutrition information and meal-planning support only. For personal medical needs, allergies or prescribed nutrition targets, speak to a qualified professional.

Do I have to pay anything for the 7-day plan?

No. The 7-day meal plan is free to generate on this page. Emailing the plan and shopping list to yourself is optional, and the full Hearty app is a separate once-off purchase.

What if Hearty is not right for me?

The full Hearty app has a 14-day money-back guarantee. If it is not useful for you, contact us within 14 days and we will refund your purchase.

Disclaimer: Hearty provides general nutrition information and practical meal-planning support. It is not medical advice and does not replace guidance from a doctor, pharmacist, prescriber or dietitian, especially for anyone using GLP-1 medication.
