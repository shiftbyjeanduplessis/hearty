Before you start

Here is what your plan is trying to protect

When appetite drops, it is easy to eat too little, miss protein, or avoid foods because you feel unsure. A useful GLP-1 plan should make the next meal feel clear, gentle and repeatable.

  • Protein at every meal. Smaller meals still need to do a job. Protein helps support muscle, fullness and better nutrition while weight is dropping.
  • Do not skip nutrition when appetite is low. A light yoghurt bowl, eggs, tuna, chicken, beans or a protein drink is better than an empty day.
  • Build meals in order. Start with protein, add vegetables or fruit, then add a sensible starch if it helps energy and satisfaction.
  • Keep carbohydrates sensible, not forbidden. Oats, potatoes, rice, whole wheat wraps, beans and fruit can help meals feel normal and support energy.
  • Use snacks strategically. Protein yoghurt, cottage cheese, boiled eggs, tuna, biltong, chicken strips or a small shake can close nutrition gaps when meals feel too big.
  • Make rough days easier. If nausea or food aversion shows up, smaller, plainer meals are often easier than large, rich or greasy meals.
  • Keep it repeatable. Familiar foods and realistic portions beat complicated recipes that disappear after one motivated week.

Hearty turns these basics into daily prompts and practical choices, so you are not starting from scratch every morning.
