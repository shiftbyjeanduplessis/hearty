Why this fits The Aesthetics Institute

The site already promises integrated aesthetics, health and wellness. The app makes that promise daily.

The Aesthetics Institute positions itself as a one-stop destination where science, artistry and holistic wellness meet. A GLP-1 support app gives that positioning a practical digital layer: clients get structure between appointments, while the clinic becomes more visible, more useful and more trusted every day.

The app system

A boutique support companion for GLP-1 clients — branded around the clinic, not a generic tracker.

The strongest version is not a logo slapped onto a meal app. It is a calm, premium daily support system that gives clients structure while reinforcing The Aesthetics Institute’s role as an integrated wellness destination.

01

GLP-1 friendly meals

Simple meal plans, smaller portions, protein visibility and nausea-friendly options.

02

Support Mode

When nausea, fatigue or low appetite hits, meals and movement soften instead of the client feeling like they failed.

03

Progress tracking

Weight, photos and trends presented in a way that supports clinical conversations and client confidence.

04

Appointment prep

Symptom and progress summaries help clients arrive prepared and practitioners get a clearer picture.
